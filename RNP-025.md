## **TL;DR**

This RNP sets out the **Year 4 BME Emissions Allocations**, covering **20 December 2026 through 19 December 2027**. Year 4 emissions are scheduled to decrease from 5.9M RENDER to **4.56M RENDER**, proposed to be allocated as follows:

* **1.19M RENDER (\~26%)** for node rewards on the rendering, compute, and Salad subnets;  
* **1.54M RENDER (\~34%)** for growth initiatives, artist and AI/compute grants, with RenderCon now funded from the grants budget; and  
* **1.83M RENDER (\~40%)** for operations, R\&D, and growth initiatives.

In addition, this RNP would:

* increase node operator rewards (availability and job rewards) by **20%** on both the rendering and compute subnets, starting in the epoch beginning after final approval;  
* approve the use of up to **0.9M RENDER** from Network Treasury (reserves) for grant spending pursuant to the virtual reality immersive gallery (see Appendix 1); and  
* allow adjustments of up to 20% between Grants and Operations and/or use of up to 20% of Network Reserves at the Foundation's discretion.

## **Network / Development**

### **Salad Network Onchain Integration**

This year, through the community-approved [RNP-023](https://github.com/rendernetwork/RNPs/blob/main/RNP-023.md), we began onboarding the third subnet to the ecosystem, that of Salad Technologies, from an onchain revenue perspective. This decentralized GPU network manages compute jobs across 16,000 daily active compute suppliers (node operators) called "Chefs." Onchain revenue will be recorded in RENDER once the integration is complete, its compute job operations are managed by the team at Salad, independent and unrelated to the rendering or AI compute subnets overseen by the Render Network Foundation.

Salad expects to continue growing their service as compute demand grows. To scale their operations in a more seamless and cost efficient manner, Salad chose to bring their Chef rewards mechanism onchain with the Render Network ecosystem.

By transitioning from traditional, fiat-based payment rails to RENDER for distributing rewards to their Chefs, Salad is able to save money and reduce often-clunky intermediary steps when issuing payouts to their large network of Chefs. A portion of revenue from Salad's operations is in turn captured onchain on the Render Network ecosystem, contributing to RENDER burns.

In 2026, Salad undertook:

* **Milestone 1** – enabling RENDER for Chef rewards.  
* **Milestone 2** – enabling RENDER as a customer payment option for network users.  
* **Test phase for Milestone 3** – designing the architecture for incorporating Salad revenue into the BME model. The design began in mid-2026, and a multi-step testing process is underway that brings revenue onchain, first in an [interim test pilot](https://blog.salad.com/daily-render-burns-have-started-testing-for-milestone-3) with burns starting on 05 November 2026 and eventually fully automated.

The full implementation of milestone 3 is expected to be completed by early Q2 of 2027.

### **Dispersed – the ecosystem's AI compute subnet**

2026 saw the formal strategic rollout of Dispersed, the Render Network ecosystem's decentralized AI compute subnet. Product development as well as usage grew steadily alongside new nodes onboarding. The ecosystem's compute capabilities were highlighted through multiple case studies presented at RenderCon 2026, demonstrating the ecosystem expanding utility beyond graphics rendering.

Case studies and use cases featured were:

* **OrbitRisk, from Corpus Park Labs.** A [predictive satellite risk intelligence](https://dispersed.com/case-studies/orbitrisk-predictive-satellite-risk-intelligence) platform that combines data across orbital mechanics modeling, collision probability analysis, and cybersecurity signals to compose dynamic risk profiles for satellite operators and insurers.  
* **Evidence.Guide.** This [analytics platform](https://dispersed.com/case-studies/how-evidence-guide-uses-ai-to-predict-which-studies-will-replicate) ingests and parses scientific papers to predict whether their findings are replicable. By improving scientific study and research replicability, the team behind Evidence.Guide aims to chip away at a \$20 billion annual cost that industries face due to a lack of reliable, affordable, and methodical ways to process research at scale.  
* **GPU infrastructure for AI agents.** Sarson Funds and Manifest Network are building [AI applications for agents](https://dispersed.com/case-studies/on-demand-gpu-infra-for-ai-agents) that dynamically access distributed GPU infrastructure to power real-time, multi-model workflows. The team built Agent1, an encrypted multi-model AI assistant that embeds, organizes, and runs inference. They also built Suma, an app for inference, financial portfolio analysis, and market classification.  
* **Omniscient.** A [decentralized memory layer](https://dispersed.com/case-studies/omniscient-a-personal-data-layer-for-ai) designed to make AI assistants persistent, personalized, and user-owned. The platform is designed to give users greater control over how their AI systems operate, where workloads run, and how personal context is stored and processed over time.  
* **Bitmap.** This [project](https://bitmap.mhxalt.com/), by artist MHX, takes one bitcoin block each day and turns its transactions into a visual representation of that data. Bitmap uses both Dispersed and Render Network to streamline the daily process into a 15 minute task that would otherwise take 35 hours on local hardware or cost over 95% more using traditional hyperscaler infrastructure.

## **Increase Node Operator Rewards 20%**

Node operator rewards will increase 20% for both the rendering and compute subnets, starting in the epoch beginning after a final approval of this proposal. This includes both availability and job rewards.

### **Render Network**

Based on growing demand and natural attrition, new node operators have been onboarded in 2026 to the rendering subnet. As immersive/experiential opportunities grow, the network is expected to see ongoing, steady growth. Node operators, the backbone of the rendering subnet, will continue to be rewarded for their availability and job completion on the network, with the rewards pool increasing 20% in the epoch following a final approval of this RNP. This remains under review following the recent implementation of the new tier system - the Render Network now offers three tiers to choose from:

* **Economy:** The most affordable tier, now twice as fast as before. Ideal for jobs requiring up to 11GB of VRAM.  
* **Standard:** Replaces our Priority tier with a better speed-to-price ratio. The go-to choice for everyday jobs with up to 24GB of VRAM.  
* **Performance:** Home to our fastest nodes, with up to 14 GPUs per machine, 48GB of VRAM, and up to 768GB of RAM. Built for the most demanding jobs, including the highest resolutions and render settings.

Given the new tier system has resulted in better matching of jobs to tier type the new efficiency has resulted in the effective through capacity of the Network increasing.

### **Dispersed (the AI compute subnet)**

Based on the rapid rise of generative AI applications, the Render Network compute subnet—Dispersed—has been launched and is seeing growth. The expansion will help position the network competitively in the AI landscape.

### **Salad subnet**

RNP-023 approved the Salad subnet to drive substantial revenue growth onto the network, directly leading to RENDER burns via the BME process. GPU compute suppliers, "Chefs", on Salad will soon fully be compensated in Render, completing the economic loop for this major revenue driver.

# **Appendix 1 — Business Case: Targeted VR Content Rendering Grants Program**

**Purpose:** Accelerate adoption, generate high-impact showcases, and strengthen platform partnerships ahead of next-generation VR form factors (e.g., Meta VR Glasses launching spring 2027).

## **Opportunity**

Meta's new [Meta VR Glasses](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-vr-glasses-3d-movies-immersive-live-sports-100-grams/) (announced September 2026, shipping spring 2027 at \~\$1,299) represent a meaningful form-factor shift: \~100 g glasses-style device (5× lighter than Quest 3) tethered to a pocket puck, with 2412×2288 micro-OLED per eye (\~37 PPD), up to 120 Hz, HDR/Dolby Vision support, and a focus on cinema, live sports, productivity, and gaming. Narrower FOV is offset by higher pixel density and comfort, potentially expanding the addressable audience beyond traditional headsets.

High-quality content remains the primary bottleneck. Cinematic 180°/360° video, photorealistic interactive experiences, and high-resolution assets optimized for elevated PPD and HDR require substantial GPU compute. The VR rendering processing market is growing rapidly (one estimate places it at \~\$7.9B in 2025 with a projected \~29% CAGR toward 2032), driven by resolution, ray tracing, and immersive demands.

Render Network is uniquely positioned: a decentralized GPU marketplace supporting OctaneRender, Redshift, and Blender Cycles, already proven at extreme scales (e.g., multi-artist 18K panoramic immersive works for ARTECHOUSE's SUBMERGE: Beyond the Render produced in weeks instead of months at a fraction of traditional cost).

## **Problem Statement**

* Independent and mid-tier VR creators/studios face high rendering costs and timelines for content that fully exploits new high-PPD, HDR-capable glasses.  
* Platforms (Meta and others) need compelling native or optimized showcase content to drive device adoption and ecosystem stickiness.  
* Without visible, high-profile examples, Render Network's advantages (cost, scale, speed for high-resolution/immersive work) remain under-recognized in the VR content pipeline.

## **Proposed Initiative**

Establish a focused **VR Showcase Grants Program** (or dedicated track within the existing Render Foundation grants framework) that provides rendering credits for ambitious VR content optimized for next-gen glasses form factors.

Scope examples:

* High-resolution 180°/360° stereoscopic, cinematic or live-event experiences.  
* Photorealistic interactive or spatial storytelling pieces (leveraging foveated-aware or high-detail pipelines).  
* Cross-platform or Meta Horizon-compatible showcases that demonstrate quality/cost advantages.  
* Targeted support for creators and channels already producing for VR/AR glasses.

Grants would be structured as network credits (consistent with current artist/studio grants), with clear deliverables: finished high-quality assets, case studies, performance metrics (render time/cost vs. alternatives), and permission for promotional use. Priority given to projects with existing distribution relationships or strong potential for platform visibility.

## **Strategic Benefits for the Render Network**

* **Adoption & Usage Flywheel** — Convert grant recipients into recurring paid users. Immersive work has already shown strong product-market fit; VR-specific showcases extend this to a high-growth vertical.  
* **Platform Pitch Asset** — Produce tangible, high-visibility demos and case studies to engage Meta (and peers) on content pipelines, co-marketing, or preferred rendering infrastructure. ARTECHOUSE-style success provides a proven template for credibility.  
* **Brand & Ecosystem Positioning** — Reinforce Render as the scalable, cost-effective infrastructure for next-generation immersive content (building on 18K work, Sphere-scale history, and existing grants momentum—hundreds of artist grants already funded).  
* **Token & Network Economics** — Drive RENDER burns via subsequent paid jobs, increase node utilization, and support emissions allocation discipline already used for artist/AI grants.  
* **Timing Advantage** — Pre-position before Meta VR Glasses launch (spring 2027) so showcase content and relationships are ready when devices reach creators and early adopters.

## **Recommended Approach & Resource Needs**

* Allocate a portion of the existing grants budget (historically scaled to hundreds of grants annually via foundation emissions/treasury) to a VR showcase track—e.g., 10–25 high-impact projects in the first cycle.  
* Leverage current grants process (quick pitch → formal proposal) with added evaluation criteria: technical fit for high-PPD/HDR VR, distribution potential, and willingness to produce public case studies.  
* Pair grants with light technical support or documentation on VR-optimized workflows (engines already supported).  
* Measure success via: number of completed showcases, subsequent paid network usage by recipients, inbound interest from platforms/creators, media/partner amplification, and incremental frames rendered in immersive/VR categories.

## **Expected Outcomes**

* Portfolio of polished VR/3D-VR showcase pieces ready for pitching to Meta, other platforms, and creator communities.  
* Measurable uptick in VR-related network activity and paid conversions.  
* Strengthened narrative and relationships positioning Render Network as essential infrastructure for the glasses-form-factor era of VR.  
* Low incremental overhead by building on the mature grants program and proven immersive track record.

**Approach:** a dedicated VR showcase grants track and allocate initial funding/credits from the Foundation's grants envelope. Launch a targeted call for submissions in the near term to generate assets and relationships ahead of the Meta VR Glasses commercial window. This is a high-leverage, relatively low-risk use of grant capacity that aligns directly with network growth, token utility, and the strongest near-term market tailwind in immersive content.
